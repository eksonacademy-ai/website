3DOF simulator: ultimate immersion

Combines VR technology with three degrees of freedom motion, creating an engaging and realistic experience.

  • Wheelchair-accessible platform with a ramp.
  • Secure restraint system (chest/lap harness) for limited trunk control.
  • Hand-only control system (throttle, brake, steering).
  • Adjustable motion intensity settings to control comfort.

2DOF simulator: compact and accessible

An immersive motion experience connecting users to environments via two degrees of freedom motion.

  • Highly responsive pitch and roll mechanics without vertical heave.
  • Wheelchair-accessible with a secure stabilization system.
  • Hand-only controls and motion calibrated to avoid motion sickness.
